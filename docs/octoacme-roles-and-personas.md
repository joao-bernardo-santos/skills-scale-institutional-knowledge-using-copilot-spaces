# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and verification that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards. They collaborate with product, engineering, and stakeholders to ensure customer-ready deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain test plans and QA approaches
- Define quality metrics and acceptance criteria validation
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify and report quality risks early
- Collaborate on Definition of Done and testing standards
- Support release verification and post-deploy validation

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable features that meet customer expectations
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Improve test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and review sessions
- Weekly sync with PM and development team
- Test reports and quality dashboards
- Acceptance criteria discussions

### Interaction with Core Roles
- Works with **Developers** to define testability requirements and coordinate testing efforts
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** to validate acceptance criteria and prioritize quality concerns
- Partners with **Project Managers** to track quality metrics and escalate risks
- Coordinates with **Technical Architects** on test automation and quality standards

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define system design, scalability, and technical strategy to support project goals. They mentor engineers, review technical decisions, and manage technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define system architecture and design patterns
- Review technical designs and trade-offs
- Assess scalability, performance, and security implications
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Document technical decisions and rationale

### Goals
- Ensure systems are maintainable, scalable, and secure
- Reduce technical debt and cycle time
- Enable high-velocity, confident delivery

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code review and mentoring
- Technical risk escalation

### Interaction with Core Roles
- Guides **Developers** on architectural decisions and technical best practices
- Advises **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and impact of feature requests
- Informs **Project Managers** of technical risks and mitigation strategies
- Collaborates with **QA/Testing Leads** on automation architecture and test infrastructure

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors/Stakeholders provide business context, prioritization guidance, and approval authority. They represent customer and business interests and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Provide prioritization guidance for trade-offs
- Receive and act on status updates and escalations
- Champion the project across the organization
- Support risk mitigation and decision-making

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment
- Reduce scope creep and rework

### Typical Communication
- Monthly status updates and milestone reviews
- Executive steering committees
- Approval gates (initiation, planning, release)
- Escalation and decision-making forums

### Interaction with Core Roles
- Provides strategic direction to **Product Managers** on business priorities and success metrics
- Approves resource allocations and timelines with **Project Managers**
- Receives escalations and risk updates from the entire delivery team
- Makes final decisions on prioritization trade-offs affecting multiple teams

---

## Design Lead

### Role Summary
Design Leads own user experience, interface design, and usability validation. They collaborate with product and engineering to ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with design standards.

### Responsibilities
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Ensure accessibility and design consistency
- Review and iterate on feature designs
- Collaborate on design-related acceptance criteria
- Advocate for user needs and usability

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, delightful user experiences
- Reduce usability issues and support load
- Ensure brand and accessibility standards are met

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Sprint planning and user story refinement
- Usability testing and research findings
- Design system and standards documentation

### Interaction with Core Roles
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on feature design and user validation
- Works with **Developers** to ensure designs are implementable and accessible
- Partners with **QA/Testing Leads** to define usability acceptance criteria
- Informs **Project Managers** of design-related timeline impacts and risks
